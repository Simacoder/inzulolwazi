Southern Africa: a quick tour (sample page)

Our tour starts at Great Zimbabwe, where guides talk about trade and stone walls. Many brochures also describe the Bantu peoples and the Khoisan in a few lines, as if each were one group.

Older textbooks sometimes mention the Hamitic hypothesis, and school notes often list racial classification systems without saying who made them or why.

Another stop is the Giza plateau. A separate museum page covers the Lebombo bone and the Ishango bone, and a book list names Chancellor Williams.
